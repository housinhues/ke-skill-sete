# Talent Management Agreement

Reference file for: **Talent management agreement (Housing Hues ↔ talent, ongoing representation)** — see `SKILL.md` Step 1.

## What makes this engagement type different

This is the umbrella agreement — it establishes the representation relationship itself, not any single paid engagement. It's typically the first contract a talent signs, immediately after the onboarding form (`talent_signup` or `artist_onboarding_models`), and before any specific booking exists. Every other engagement type in this library (photoshoot, campaign, event coverage, etc.) sits *underneath* this one once it's in place — this agreement doesn't replace those, it's the container they get drafted into later.

**By design, this contract carries no fee or payment terms.** There's no paying gig to attach numbers to at signing — payment terms belong in whichever engagement-specific contract eventually applies, drafted fresh when real work exists. See "Compensation — open decision" below for the one thing this leaves unresolved.

## Fields this draws on

Pulled straight from the onboarding intake (`talent_signup` / `artist_onboarding_models` form fields) rather than a separate interview — check the applicant's submission before asking the talent to repeat anything:

- `legal_name`, `stage_name` — parties
- `dob` — **required before drafting.** If missing from the intake (it sometimes is on the short `talent_signup` form), get it before proceeding. If the talent is a minor, stop — this reference doesn't cover guardian consent or minor-specific terms, and a materially different contract structure is needed first.
- `discipline` — defines the scope of representation and feeds the exclusivity clause
- `other_management`, `existing_deals`, `prior_rep_details` — check these before granting any exclusivity; a "No" on other_management with something listed under existing_deals is a contradiction worth resolving before signing, not drafting around
- `instagram` / `tiktok` / `social_link` — relevant to the social media tier clause
- `id_number`, banking fields, `emergency_contact` — kept on the talent's internal file (Drive folder, once created), not written into the agreement text. These aren't contract terms; they're operational records for when payment eventually does flow.

## Non-negotiable clauses

Pulled as-is from `clause-library.md`, with one modification flagged:

- **Governing law & dispute resolution** — AFSA arbitration, seated in South Africa. As-is.
- **Confidentiality** — as-is, 2-year survival period.
- **IP / content ownership defaults** — as-is. Note this covers portfolio/promotional content Housing Hues might use of the talent even before any paid deal exists (see Usage rights below) — it's not just for booked work.
- **Brand-safety conduct (minimum standard)** — as-is, applies from signing, not from first paid gig.
- **Sponsored-content disclosure** — as-is. No live sponsorship yet, but this sets the standing expectation the talent is bound to the moment one exists.
- **Termination for cause — modified for this type.** The clause-library default ties cause to "non-payment past 14 days." A pure management agreement with no payment mechanism has no obvious non-payment event to trigger on. Two ways this resolves, depending on the Compensation decision below:
  - If Housing Hues ends up taking no standing commission (compensation fully deferred to per-engagement contracts): drop the non-payment ground entirely from *this* contract; it lives instead in whichever engagement contract actually moves money.
  - If Housing Hues does take a standing commission on secured work: the non-payment ground applies to that commission going unpaid past 14 days of an engagement's own payout.
  Brand-safety breach and illegal activity remain valid cause regardless.
- **Document versioning** — as-is.

## Negotiable clauses (scoped to this type)

- **Usage rights** — scoped narrower than a campaign's usage clause: limited to Housing Hues using the talent's submitted portfolio content and likeness for its own roster marketing (website, Instagram, pitch decks, proposals), not for any specific brand deal. Time-bound to the life of the management relationship — usage rights end when representation ends, not before.
- **Exclusivity scope** — defined by `discipline`. A talent onboarded for Modeling only isn't automatically exclusive for Music too, if that's a separate pursuit. Cross-check against `other_management`/`existing_deals` before granting anything broader than the disclosed disciplines.
- **Social media posting tier (Managed vs Independent)** — explicit, signed, dated choice at signing, per clause-library. This is a standing choice for the relationship, not renegotiated per post.
- **Renewal / extension terms** — opt-in default, per clause-library.
- **Termination without cause** — replaces the clause-library's Cancellation/Kill-Fee term, which doesn't apply here (no standing fee exists to scale a kill-fee against at this level). Instead: either party may terminate the management relationship with 30 days' written notice, no cause required. Any engagement already underway at the time of notice is unaffected and completes under its own contract's terms.

## Compensation — open decision, not yet settled

Per your instruction, no fee or payment fields are collected during onboarding, and this agreement doesn't negotiate money for work that doesn't exist yet. That part's settled.

What's still open: does Housing Hues take a **standing commission** (a flat % stated once, here, that applies to any paid engagement it secures for the talent going forward) — or is Housing Hues' cut also negotiated fresh inside each engagement-specific contract, with this agreement saying nothing about money at all?

I haven't defaulted this either way — a management agreement with zero mention of how the agency gets paid is unusual enough that it's worth a deliberate call rather than a guess. The worked example below leaves it bracketed. Let me know which way to draft it and I'll lock the clause in.

**HOUSING HUES TALENT MANAGEMENT AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is entered into on [DATE] between:

**Housing Hues** ("the Agency"), and
**Palesa Nkosi**, professionally known as **"Pali"** ("the Talent")

**1. Representation.** The Agency is appointed to represent the Talent in the discipline(s) of: Modeling. This appointment is non-exclusive unless Clause 3 states otherwise.

**2. Term.** This Agreement begins on [DATE] and continues until terminated under Clause 8. [Renewal: opt-in only — this Agreement does not auto-renew.]

**3. Exclusivity.** The Talent confirms no existing management or representation for the discipline(s) covered by this Agreement, per their onboarding disclosure. Representation under this Agreement is exclusive to Housing Hues for Modeling only; the Talent remains free to pursue or be represented separately in any other discipline not listed here.

**4. Usage rights.** The Agency may use the Talent's submitted portfolio content and likeness for its own promotional purposes — website, social media, pitch materials — for the duration of this Agreement. This right ends when this Agreement terminates and does not extend to any specific brand or campaign use, which requires its own separate agreement.

**5. Social media posting.** The Talent is on the [Managed / Independent] tier. [If Managed: the Agency holds approval rights over public-facing, brand-relevant posts.] [If Independent: the Talent retains full posting control.] Both tiers remain subject to Clause 6 regardless.

**6. Brand-safety conduct.** The Talent agrees not to publish or engage in conduct that disparages the Agency, breaches any active exclusivity under this Agreement, or violates the sponsored-content disclosure requirement in Clause 7.

**7. Sponsored-content disclosure.** Any paid or sponsored content the Talent publishes must be disclosed per applicable advertising standards (including the ASA Code) at the time of posting.

**8. Termination.**
&nbsp;&nbsp;(a) *For cause* — either party may terminate immediately for: conduct breaching Clause 6, illegal activity, [or non-payment of Agency commission past 14 days of an engagement payout, if Clause 10 establishes a standing commission].
&nbsp;&nbsp;(b) *Without cause* — either party may terminate with 30 days' written notice. Any engagement already underway at the time of notice completes under its own separate contract.

**9. Confidentiality.** Neither party will disclose the other's business terms, unreleased content, or internal communications without consent. This obligation survives termination for 2 years.

**10. Compensation.** [OPEN — pending decision: either "The Agency's commission on any engagement it secures for the Talent is X% of the gross booking fee, payable on the Talent's receipt of payment" or "This Agreement establishes no standing compensation terms; all compensation is defined in the engagement-specific contract for each booking."]

**11. Intellectual property.** Ownership of any content created during the Talent's engagements is governed by the IP clause in the relevant engagement-specific contract. This Agreement itself creates no content ownership beyond the limited usage right in Clause 4.

**12. Governing law.** This Agreement is governed by the law of the Republic of South Africa. Disputes are resolved by binding arbitration under AFSA rules, seated in South Africa.
